About Waload Limited

Who We Are

WALOAD LIMITED is a diversified company delivering innovative solutions across technology, media, real estate, professional training, agriculture, and transport. Established with a commitment to excellence, integrity, and customer satisfaction.

Through our divisions — WALOAD TECH, WALOAD PHOTO & VIDEOGRAPHY, WALOAD ACADEMICS, WALOAD REAL ESTATE, WALOAD AGRO, WALOAD LINE, and WALOAD ESSENTIALS — we offer wide-ranging solutions that create value and support sustainable development.

Meet Our CEO

Visionary leadership driving excellence and innovation

Igbeleke Olumide David

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Igbeleke Olumide David is a highly skilled and versatile professional with expertise in petroleum technology, software engineering, data analysis, and cybersecurity. He holds a Higher National Diploma in Petroleum Engineering and Geoscience from the Petroleum Training Institute and is certified in Software, Data, and Cybersecurity Engineering from Newdich Technology.

Under his visionary leadership, Waload Limited has grown into a diversified conglomerate with interests in technology, media, real estate, training, agriculture, and transport. His commitment to adaptability, integrity, and innovation has been the driving force behind the company's success.

Olumide is also a passionate mentor and has trained individuals in web development, data analysis, and cybersecurity. He believes in using technology to solve real-world problems and is dedicated to contributing to sustainable development across Africa.
